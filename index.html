About Center-ECD Academy

Practical training, built for the African professional

Center-ECD Academy is the training arm of the Center for Enterprise and Capacity Development — a professional learning platform built to help individuals and organisations develop the skills that matter in today's world.

We combine expert instruction, real-world application, and verifiable certification to give you credentials that actually open doors — whether you're starting your career, levelling up, or building your own business.
